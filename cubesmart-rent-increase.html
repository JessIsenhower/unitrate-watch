How to evaluate the increase

1. Identify what price actually changed

Separate base monthly rent from insurance/protection, administrative fees, taxes, and any expiring promotion. Otherwise you may compare two different bundles.

2. Compare the online and in-store pricing carefully

CubeSmart location pages can display discounted online prices alongside higher in-store prices. Record both when available and note the promotion terms before treating either as the long-term market rate.

3. Match the physical unit

Climate control, elevator or stair access, drive-up access, floor, and actual dimensions matter. CubeSmart itself notes that actual unit sizes can vary from approximate advertised dimensions.

4. Negotiate before taking on moving costs

If your new base rate is materially above a credible comparable range, ask what existing-customer adjustment is available. Then compare any remaining gap with the cost and inconvenience of moving.

Common questions

Can CubeSmart raise my rate?

CubeSmart's current public promotion terms say the initial monthly rate may be adjusted at any time with 30 days' advance notice. Your own rental agreement and applicable law determine the terms that apply to you.

Why is the online price lower than what I pay?

CubeSmart publicly offers new-customer promotions and online discounts on selected units. That does not make the price irrelevant, but it means the comparison needs to account for promotion duration, unit attributes, and excluded charges.

Should I compare the total bill or base rent?

Start with base rent, then separately compare recurring protection/insurance and required fees. CubeSmart's terms explicitly say advertised pricing can exclude several charges.

When is switching worth it?

When the sustainable monthly savings, multiplied by how long you expect to need storage, comfortably exceed the cost and hassle of moving. A concierge scan helps estimate the realistic range rather than relying on the cheapest headline price.
