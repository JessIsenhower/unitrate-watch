What the scan answers

Stay, negotiate, or switch?

The goal is a decision, not a pile of links.

Check your current facility first

Is the same or a similar unit being advertised materially below what you now pay?

Check credible nearby alternatives

We compare relevant size, climate control, access, floor, and location rather than grabbing the cheapest headline rate.

Separate promotions from durable pricing

An introductory offer can still save money, but it should not be mistaken for a guaranteed long-term rate.

Put moving friction into the decision

A $20 monthly gap may not justify a move. A $100 gap might. The answer depends on the actual economics.

What we are testing

Will renters act when the math becomes visible?

Recent consumer discussions repeatedly show the same pain: sharp increases, cheaper advertised rates at the same facility, and uncertainty about whether negotiating or moving is worth it. UnitRate Watch is testing whether an independent market check removes enough friction to be useful.
