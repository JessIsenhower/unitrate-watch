# UnitRate Watch — Affiliate Monetization Plan

## Primary hypothesis
Keep the consumer utility free. Monetize qualified reservations or referrals when a renter chooses an alternative after comparing the market.

## Guardrails
- Affiliate compensation must be disclosed near monetized recommendations.
- Commission must not be the sole determinant of ranking.
- Non-affiliate options may still be shown when they are relevant.
- Never imply a rate is guaranteed; require confirmation with the provider.
- Track clicks, qualifying reservations and revenue separately from scan-request conversion.
- Do not enroll in programs aimed at storage operators merely because they use the word “affiliate.”

## First candidate
Neighbor: investigate/apply to its consumer-facing affiliate program. Public program materials currently describe compensation for qualifying new-renter reservations.

## Next candidates
Research storage marketplaces and comparison platforms with publisher/referral partnerships, then direct operator programs where available.

## Owner action boundary
Applications requiring legal name, tax information, payout account, contractual acceptance or identity verification require explicit owner action. Do not submit those fields autonomously.
