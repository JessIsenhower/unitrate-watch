NYC opened a $1 million restitution fund for some Extra Space Storage customers.

If you rented an Extra Space unit in New York City during the covered period and experienced certain rate increases, property damage, or auction problems, check the city’s official eligibility page directly.

Official DCWP criteria

Who may qualify

New York City’s Department of Consumer and Worker Protection says potential claimants must have rented an Extra Space Storage unit in New York City between March 24, 2023 and June 16, 2026 and experienced at least one covered problem.

  • Rent increased within the first six months of renting, or by more than 50% during the rental.
  • Belongings were damaged because of conditions such as rodents, mold, leaks, bugs, or other unsafe or unclean conditions.
  • Belongings were sold or auctioned without proper notice, or while the renter was disputing a rent increase or other fees.

Meeting these conditions does not guarantee payment. DCWP reviews claims and determines eligibility.

Deadline

DCWP currently says the deadline is August 1, 2027, or until the settlement fund runs out.

Why the fund exists

New York City announced a $1.7 million settlement with Extra Space in July 2026, including $1 million in consumer restitution and more than $700,000 in civil penalties. The city said its investigation reviewed more than 100 complaints and alleged deceptive pricing and other consumer-protection violations.
