Privacy Notice

Effective August 20, 2026.

UnitRate Watch is an independent self-storage price-transparency experiment. We designed the beta to work without collecting your name, email address, account number, unit number, gate code, payment information, or other identifying account details.

What we collect

If you request a market scan, we collect the facility or ZIP code, current and previous rent you provide, unit size and type, advertised rate if provided, optional comparison context, submission time, and operational data generated by our hosting provider.

Why we collect it

We use submitted information to evaluate storage pricing, research comparable publicly advertised options, operate and improve UnitRate Watch, prevent abuse, and measure whether the service is useful.

Where it is processed

The website is hosted by Vercel and scan-request data is stored using Neon Postgres. These service providers process information as part of operating the site and database.

Retention

During the beta, scan-request data is retained only as long as reasonably useful for providing and evaluating the service. We intend to periodically delete stale beta submissions rather than build permanent consumer profiles.

Affiliate links

UnitRate Watch may eventually use affiliate or referral links. If you make a qualifying reservation through one of those links, UnitRate Watch may receive compensation. Affiliate relationships will not change the price you pay and should not determine how options are ranked.

Please do not submit private information

Do not enter names, email addresses, account or unit numbers, gate codes, payment information, or sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

This notice may change as the beta evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
